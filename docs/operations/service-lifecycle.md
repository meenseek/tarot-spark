# Service Lifecycle

## Scope

Use this procedure to connect a product decision to its release, observation,
support, recovery, or closure. Keep execution evidence with the actual change,
campaign, incident, or provider record. This document owns the operational
handoff; it does not set new growth thresholds, commerce terms, or schedules.

## Choose The Next Decision

1. Read the [growth playbook](../product/growth-playbook.md#decision-sequence)
   and [revenue validation plan](../product/revenue-validation-plan.md). Bind the
   selected goal, current phase, next gate, and proceed, hold, or stop criteria
   to their owner source and the current observation period.
2. Check the active decision run and its campaign register before selecting
   another publishing or activation candidate. Missing attribution, samples,
   or required observations leave the decision pending. Reach, product use,
   interest, and settled sales each retain their own gate.
3. Select the next change within the existing scope, resource limits, and
   authorization. Use [phase-gated delivery](../workflow/delivery-phases.md)
   for the exact plan and candidate. A gate pass does not grant publishing,
   deployment, checkout, or new recurring work authorization.

## Apply And Observe

1. Bind the reviewed candidate and configuration to the target environment.
   Confirm concurrent changes before applying it. Repository integration and
   production deployment are separate results.
2. After an authorized release, confirm the deployed candidate and production
   origin using the deployment provider's existing readback. Follow the
   [configuration contract](../../README.md#configuration).
3. Check home to reading, result to prompt copy, and the selected return or
   share path in the affected locales and device widths. Check opt-out and
   opt-in against the existing analytics and advertising guardrails. Check an
   enabled feature's failure and recovery path without sending user content
   to analytics. A page response alone does not prove the journey works.
4. Observe the exact candidate, attribution, period, and denominator required
   by the selected gate. Record a decision and next action in the existing
   change or campaign result. Missing business observations remain pending;
   successful delivery or tests do not establish demand or revenue.

## Handle Support

Use the published [Contact page](<../../src/app/(root)/contact/page.tsx>) and its
localized [public-page copy](../../src/messages/en/public-pages.json) as the
current intake boundary. Read the actual request only in the approved mailbox
or support record. Do not copy addresses, personal questions, or tarot context
to Git, analytics, campaign records, or company reports.

1. Identify the affected version and journey, the requested response, and any
   privacy, delivery, or payment obligation. Record the minimum non-identifying
   problem and a source pointer where an existing authorized record permits it.
2. Reproduce a site issue with synthetic input and use the normal delivery
   gates for a fix. Confirm the corrected journey and authorized response
   before treating the request as resolved. Sending a response requires the
   existing communication authorization.
3. Handle deletion or privacy requests through the actual retained data and
   [privacy copy](../../src/messages/en/public-pages.json), including any
   processor involved. A free-reading fix does not prove email deletion or a
   provider refund. Recheck each requested effect at its owner.
4. Before paid launch, use the revenue plan's commerce-readiness gate to
   establish the real provider, fulfillment, support owner, response terms,
   refunds, receipts, and data obligations. If these are unresolved, keep
   checkout unpublished. Do not invent a response deadline or refund policy.

## Recover A Service Incident

1. Confirm the actual impact, environment, deployed candidate, and affected
   path. Distinguish provider delay, missing measurement, a broken journey,
   access failure, and a suspected customer-data issue.
2. Use the existing deployment or configuration owner to prepare the smallest
   recovery within the current authorization. Bind a rollback to the known
   prior candidate and configuration; do not undo unrelated releases. Read
   back an ambiguous deployment before retrying it.
3. Keep analytics, advertising, and instant-reading enablement within their
   existing [guardrails](../product/guardrails.md) and configuration gates.
   New credentials, retention changes, destructive data actions, spending,
   or an unauthorized external effect require the specific owner decision.
4. Recheck the deployed candidate and affected journey, consent behavior,
   provider state, and remaining support obligations after recovery. Record
   unresolved effects separately. The incident remains open for those effects
   even when the site responds again.

## Propose And Execute Closure

Prepare closure from the applicable product stop criterion and actual
observations. Separate insufficient samples and a recoverable incident from
a business stop decision. Present the alternatives, customer and consumer
impact, and the exact scope requiring the owner's decision.

After explicit closure authorization, handle each applicable boundary and
read back the result:

- Customers and commerce: resolve or transfer outstanding support, fulfillment,
  refunds, chargebacks, receipts, and provider obligations. Verify actual retained
  records even when the app has no checkout.
- Distribution and recurring work: identify actual campaign slots, native
  schedules, collectors, and missing-report monitors. Pause the authorized
  targets and confirm settings and active deployments with their owners. Shared
  company reporting and other channels retain their own boundaries.
- Site and dependencies: confirm the authorized site, origin, routes, and
  consuming services after the approved shutdown or replacement. Code archival
  does not prove traffic or consumers have been handled.
- Access and data: apply the approved revocation, processor, retention, deletion,
  or asset-transfer scope and read back each effect. Preserve obligations and
  required evidence. Closure alone does not authorize deletion or a new backup.

Use the campaign and channel owners for social publication, production assets,
and collector changes. Preserve their existing approval and storage boundaries.
Leave unresolved effects with their exact owner, evidence, and next action;
confirm all authorized applicable effects before reporting service closure.

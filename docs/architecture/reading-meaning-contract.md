# Reading Meaning Contract

The localized reviewed meaning is shared by card text, copied prompts and the
server request. It is a product interpretation of an upright card within a
symbolic reading, not verified knowledge of a person or event.

## Candidate content revision

The 2026-11-04 candidate preserves the High Priestess’s intuition, discretion
and
careful listening while adding an inner reserve that is not quickly disclosed.
It preserves the Nine of Wands’ fatigued persistence and resilience while adding
vigilance and holding one’s ground. These are narrow editorial expansions, not
a claim that all other meanings are defective.

Waite’s descriptions include silence for the High Priestess and watchful
resistance for the Nine of Wands. Those passages support the candidate’s
editorial choices; they are not empirical evidence about users or other people.
See [The Pictorial Key to the Tarot, Part III](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot/Part_3),
the Nine of Wands entry and the Greater Arcana’s High Priestess entry. The
product does not adopt the book’s literal predictions, demographic assignments
or depicted scenes as facts about a reading.

The Page of Swords retains careful verification and honest communication while
adding alert watchfulness. Waite’s Page of Swords entry includes vigilance and
examination; the candidate adapts that nuance without importing spying, an enemy
or private monitoring. It is not evidence of any actual person’s hidden
activity.

Reserve must not become an assertion of secrets or infidelity. Vigilance must
not become an invented trauma, enemy, diagnosis or past event. The surrounding
tarot frame and the existing safety boundaries continue to apply.

## Evidence when meanings change

Keep every earlier answer with its original supplied meanings. A content edit
does not retroactively invalidate or improve that attempt’s semantic score.
For paired development review, grade each answer against its own preserved
materials. Use separate anonymous inputA/inputB fields when materials differ;
retain the original common input field when they are identical. Compare the
result as a before/after product revision with changed content, not an isolated
model or generation-method improvement. The six scoring dimensions and all
existing acceptance thresholds remain unchanged.

A cached development assessment requires the full provider body, display input,
actual answer, reference, blind case, rubric, both complete original reader
files and entries, generation and transport contracts to remain equivalent.
Hash equality is an identity check, not semantic evidence. Preserve any required
independent conflict decision with the original reader files before reusing a
resolved assessment. A meaning edit
invalidates requests selecting that card; unrelated cards do not acquire the
new nuance. Recompute worst-case request sizes and the fixed release reserve
from all 78 current definitions before any provider call.

Only a passing full development cohort may advance to final CLI checks and a
fresh 100-answer release evaluation. Release review uses the current meanings
and the original absolute quality gates; development caches do not replace it.
The owner’s final review must include the changed meanings and actual readings
before activation or deployment. The current content experiment is not a
completed release evaluation. The narrowly scoped
[owner-directed publication](../product/instant-reading-evaluation.md#owner-directed-publication)
decision records the current deployment choice separately from these unchanged
historical quality results and future evaluation requirements.

## Question answer timing

Open the story through the first supplied meaning and introduce every compound
core in drawn order. A verdict that depends on a later meaning belongs where
that meaning first contributes; do not summarize later emotions or judgments
in an opening verdict. The answer must still address the actual person,
action, tense and comparison, without a meaning lecture or closing summary.

The internal `change-evidence` writing profile is selected only for
`evidence-of-change`. It answers whether the supplied meanings support grounds
for a reunion to differ and separates future potential from an already-made
change. The `preparation` profile continues to answer what to check. Neither
profile creates evidence, conditions or tensions absent from the materials.
Choice readings preserve the actual alternatives, and opportunity-cost
readings name forgone room only when supported. Missing or insufficient
grounds are valid answers; do not manufacture a loss to satisfy the question.

The prospective provider candidate uses 1,200 output tokens with the existing
low reasoning effort and 30-second deadline. This is a generation-capacity
change, not a content-gate change or a refund of earlier evaluations. A
truncated or unavailable first attempt still fails its planned cohort; no
retry, denominator reduction or favorable rescoring is permitted.

## Question material and writing policy

Question focus carries the question being answered. Editorial safeguards belong
in the selected writing policy and remain in both copied and provider prompts.
The opportunity-cost focus is its existing title; its actual-terms,
current-work-ending and per-meaning-loss prohibitions stay in the selected
opportunity-cost policy. Consumer assertions verify the rule once in that
policy and the full copied prompt rather than requiring policy in the question.

Apply those safeguards while writing without reciting prohibitions or
explaining interpretations that were not made. A missing-ground or
insufficient-cost answer still directly answers the actual question. Preserve
every compound meaning's relational subject and scope. When the question asks
who acts first, establish the supported actor or uncertainty through the first
meaning; do not invent an actor or a fixed card position. These are prospective
writing constraints, with all quality and first-attempt gates unchanged.

## Person-led prose candidate

The shared localized narration contract describes people’s feelings, judgments
and choices with plain verbs. It excludes commentary about meanings attaching,
attitudes being placed or the arrangement of a reading. Every compound meaning
and its relationship scope still contributes in first substantive draw order.
An answer supported only by a later meaning waits for that meaning; cost
questions keep the trade-off in focus without a long introduction of psychology
and benefits, invented external alternatives or a premature cost verdict.
The API and copied prompt use the same narration and selected profile policy.
This is an unselected candidate, with the original evaluation gates unchanged.

## Question-owned writing policies

An optional localized public-question policy overrides its generic writing
profile only after the existing question/topic validation. Broad readings and
questions without an own override retain their existing overview or profile.
The shared resolver supplies both copied and provider prompts; examples,
reviewed meanings, observer and common narration retain their existing owners.
Contact and effort question focus contains only its title, with editorial
constraints in its selected policy. A balanced effort reading need not invent
a limit; limits are described only when supported by the supplied meanings.

## Traits in the question’s context

Preserve a supplied strength without turning it into an automatic deficiency.
A trait can still shape a choice or tension in the actual question: wanting
closeness and keeping one’s own pace can coexist. This contextual symbolic
reading does not assert an external event, verified private cause or history.
When grounds are genuinely absent, answer the question briefly within that
limit; do not retract a supported interpretation with a verification lecture.
Effort balance distinguishes the amount of work from how fully it feels
received, and addresses that question through the first meaning where supported,
without a long introduction about feelings. A verdict supported only by a
later meaning still waits until that meaning first contributes. Compound
meaning cores and first-use order, scope, safety and every evaluation gate retain
their existing requirements.
